Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Are market prices the same as my account performance?

No. Market prices are third-party reference data. Your account balance is calculated only from ledger entries recorded for your account.

How are deposits confirmed?

Every account has its own unique deposit addresses — always use the address shown in your account for the selected asset and network. Incoming transfers are tracked on the blockchain and credited after the required network confirmations.

How do withdrawals work?

Submit the amount, network and destination address. Each request is checked, then paid with its blockchain transaction hash recorded, so you can verify the transfer yourself on any explorer. Payout timing depends on network conditions.

How are profits generated?

ZentraChain runs automated trading systems that monitor the markets around the clock for opportunities. The percentages shown on plans are targets based on that activity — not guaranteed outcomes. All trading carries risk, including possible loss of capital.

Does the platform guarantee returns?

No. Cryptocurrency trading is high risk. There are no guaranteed profits, and historical results do not predict future results.

Is exchange trading automated?

No exchange API is currently connected to this portal. The market chart is informational and does not execute trades.

How do I contact support?

Use the support contact supplied through your verified company communication channel. Do not trust unsolicited wallet addresses or messages claiming to be support.
