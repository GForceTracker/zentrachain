Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Are market prices the same as my account performance?

No. Market prices are third-party reference data. Your account balance is calculated only from ledger entries recorded for your account.

How are deposits confirmed?

Submit a deposit request with the network and transaction hash. An administrator must independently verify the transaction before a ledger credit is recorded. A submitted request is not a confirmed deposit.

How do withdrawals work?

Submit the amount, network and destination address. Requests are reviewed. A withdrawal is marked paid only after the transfer has been made and its transaction hash recorded.

Does the platform guarantee returns?

No. Cryptocurrency trading is high risk. There are no guaranteed profits, and historical results do not predict future results.

Is exchange trading automated?

No exchange API is currently connected to this portal. The market chart is informational and does not execute trades.

How do I contact support?

Use the support contact supplied through your verified company communication channel. Do not trust unsolicited wallet addresses or messages claiming to be support.
